## Theme 1: EPD as a Network of Democracy Support Organisations

**Theme summary:** EPD (European Partnership for Democracy) is a network of organisations working on democracy support. Thijs's interview explores EPD's trajectory — its membership growth (21 members), governance structure, legal form, and adaptation to democratic backsliding in Europe. The interviewer frames the conversation around the Nets4DEM book's core questions: how networks transform their membership, governance, legal status, and funding in response to democratic stress. Thijs begins by asking which other networks are being studied, suggesting a comparative interest.

**Detailed explanation:** EPD functions as both a membership organisation and an operational entity with a Brussels-based secretariat. Its members are democracy support organisations across Europe. The network provides collective advocacy in Brussels (where most members lack representation), facilitates access to EU funding, and increasingly produces research (the Democracy Hub with Carnegie). The governance includes a board and the position of President (currently Anu Juvonen of DEMO Finland). Thijs's acting executive director role places him at the operational centre of the network.

**Specific elements:**
- Actors: EPD (21 members), Carnegie Endowment, EU institutions.
- Territories: Brussels (secretariat/advocacy), Europe.
- Practices: Collective advocacy, EU funding access, research production.

**Key quotations:** *"Which other networks then do you research or do you speak with?"* (IFP20, Thijs Berman) — indicating comparative interest.
